Famous Actor, First Book
LONDON (25 February)—Fans of Simon
Eklund will be delighted with his
autobiography, The Theatre Lights Dimmed,
the first book he has written in his storied
career as an actor. It provides wonderful
insight into his career, starting with his first
roles in cinema in his native Sweden, moving
into his work in France and Italy, and finishing
with his recent theatre work in the U.K.
In his book, Mr. Eklund dedicates a fair
amount of text to discussing his mentor,
Charles Gunnarsson, who helped him develop
his skills early on in Stockholm. He also
describes the difficulty of transitioning into
different types of roles, especially from
comedic to dramatic acting. He includes
several funny anecdotes about his first
attempts at acting onstage here in London. He
describes them as disastrous, but anyone who
saw his recent performance in Life and Games
would say just the opposite.
Mr. Eklund has long been a captivating actor
on stage and screen, and now he is a
thoroughly engaging author.
—Uma Joshi
