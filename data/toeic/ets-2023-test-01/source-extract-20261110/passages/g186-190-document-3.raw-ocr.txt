Wonder Ridge Radio Job Opening: Programming Assistant
Posted November 2
Job Description
The programming assistant reports to the director of programming and supports the
radio station by performing a variety of research and communication functions. This
role is an entry-level, part-time position.
Responsibilities
• Conducting background research on interviewees
• Keeping up-to-date on news and news makers in order to suggest potential topics
and guests for on-air interviews
• Updating the station's Web site and program host biography pages
• Using scheduling software to update the broadcast schedule
• mmunicating with listeners, especially via e-mail and social media
To apply, e-mail a résumé and cover letter to hiring@wonderridgeradio.org.
